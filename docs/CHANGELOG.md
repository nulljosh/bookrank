# Changelog

Every tagged version of Kernel, newest first. Before 2026-10-28 the app was called Bookrank, and before that Uprighty, Spine and Books.

## 57.0.0 (2026-10-29)
With a public profile turned on, your shared summaries can show up in search; without it they stay out, as before.

## 56.0.0 (2026-10-29)
The landing tells the current story: every way to bring books in, narration in four languages, real iPhone screens, and what Goodreads does for you. Fresh English store screenshots.

## 55.0.0 (2026-10-29)
A shared summary link now shows up as a card with the book's title and cover in Messages, Slack and X, instead of a bare link.

## 54.0.0 (2026-10-29)
A public profile, if you want one: turn it on in your profile and bookrank.jaybulb.com/profile?u=<your name> shows the books you already shared by link, and your highlights from them if you choose. Off by default.

## 53.0.0 (2026-10-29)
Pick the hour your morning line arrives, so nothing comes before you are up, and turn on a weekly recap with your minutes and listening days.

## 52.0.0 (2026-10-29)
Account is grouped the way you use it: Listening, Goodreads, Appearance, Notifications and Data, with Storage reachable from Data.

## 51.0.0 (2026-10-29)
Clearer empty and error states: a library that cannot load says so in plain words with a Try again button, and empty Highlights, an empty library and a search with no match each say what to do next. In all four languages.

## 50.0.0 (2026-10-29)
The first-run pages offer "Bring my Goodreads shelves": Goodreads' own sign-in, and your shelves fill the library. The pages themselves now translate.

## 49.0.0 (2026-10-28)
A new store listing in English, French, Spanish and German, with iPhone and iPad screenshots in each language, ready for the next version. Two buttons that stayed English in the apps now translate.

## 48.0.0 (2026-10-28)
"Explain it" narrates in your language: French, Spanish or German, following the app's or the browser's language, whatever language the notes are in.

## 47.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Kernel speaks French, Spanish and German: the apps follow the system language and the website follows the browser's. Your summaries stay as you wrote them.

## 46.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Accessibility: chapters follow the largest text sizes in Settings, long chapter titles wrap instead of cutting off, the Mac menu bar player names its buttons for VoiceOver, and Reduce Motion stops slides and springs everywhere.

## 45.0.0 (2026-10-28)
The sleep timer fades the voice out over its last 30 seconds instead of cutting off, shaking the iPhone gives it ten more minutes, and the menu says when it will pause.

## 44.0.0 (2026-10-28)
A Storage screen in the apps, under Your reading: how much room each book's narration takes, removed with a swipe or a right-click, plus the search index and the offline library.

## 43.0.0 (2026-10-28)
On Wi-Fi, the apps fetch the next chapter's narration while you listen, so the book keeps going through a tunnel or a flight. Never on cellular or in Low Data Mode.

## 42.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Pick the narrator: Alice or George, remembered for each book like the speed, in the apps' chapter menu and the web player.

## 41.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Your summaries over the API and MCP: list, read, add and change them from a script or an agent with your Kernel sign-in token (docs/API.md).

## 40.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Spotlight: search your iPhone or Mac for a line or a title and your Kernel books and highlights come up; a result opens the book.

## 39.0.0 (2026-10-28)
A Listen menu on the Mac with every listening shortcut, and ⌘K to find a book or an action by typing. Both work from an iPad keyboard too.

## 38.0.0 (2026-10-28)
A mini player in the Mac menu bar while a book is open: the chapter, play or pause, a line back or forward, and the next chapter.

## 37.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Handoff: the book open on your iPhone shows up on your Mac's Dock (and the other way), and opens in the web library on a device without the app.

## 36.0.0 (2026-10-28)
"Continue my book in Kernel": Siri opens the book you are partway through and plays from where you stopped.

## 35.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Shortcuts and Siri: Add a summary from any text, Play or pause, and Next chapter, in the Shortcuts app and by voice ("Next chapter in Kernel").

## 34.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Keep the exact words: Add pages in the apps can read each page on your device, word for word, so the photo never leaves it. Summarize them still works the old way.

## 33.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Scan pages in Add pages on iPhone and iPad: the document camera finds each page's edges, flattens it and reads it, no photo library needed.

## 32.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Bring your own ebooks: an EPUB without DRM becomes a book with a chapter per section, on the web and in the apps, and the apps take PDFs too, split by the PDF's own chapters.

## 31.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Readwise highlights: choose a Readwise CSV export where you import Kindle highlights, and each book gets a Readwise highlights chapter.

## 30.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Kindle highlights: plug your Kindle in and choose My Clippings.txt, under the library on the web or in Add pages in the apps. Each book gets a Kindle highlights chapter, or becomes a new book, and only lines you do not have yet are added.

## 29.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Goodreads on the web: export your library from Goodreads and choose the file under the library. Your shelves show up beside your summaries, read in your browser and never sent to Kernel.

## 28.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Share your year: under Your reading, a gold card with the year, the books you finished and summarized, your highlights, the hours, and one line you saved.

## 27.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Your reading, under the library: how many books, chapters, hours to listen, highlights and notes. With Goodreads signed in, the apps also chart the books you finished each year and your average rating.

## 26.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Long-press or swipe a Goodreads book in the apps and pick Summarize this book: Add pages opens with its title already filled in.

## 25.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Your own note under every chapter, saved as you type and synced to your other devices. Notes come along in the Markdown export with your highlights.

## 24.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Today's five: five of your highlights come back at the top of the library each day, the same five on every device, a new five tomorrow. Done puts them away until then.

## 23.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Export on the Highlights page saves every highlight as one Markdown file, by book and chapter, ready for Notes, Obsidian or anywhere else.

## 22.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Highlights: every line you saved, from every book, on one page. Tap one to open the book at that line, or share it as a square gold card with the book's name.

## 21.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Search by meaning: below the word matches, Close in meaning finds the lines that say what you typed in other words, and opens the book at that line. Each book is read once and kept on the device.

## 20.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Sort the library by recently edited, title, progress or shortest first, and show all books, the ones in progress or the ones not started. Each device remembers your pick.

## 19.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Collections: put a book in a group of your own from its page, and the library lists your collections A to Z, then everything else. The same on the apps and the web.

## 18.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Search finds words inside your chapters, not only titles. Each result shows the chapter and the line that matched, and opening it lands on that line, marked, in the apps and on the web.

## 17.0.0 (2026-10-28)
Add pages on the web works like the apps: a progress bar while each photo is read, the pages that could not be read named, and the new text selected in the editor so you can fix it before you save.

## 16.0.0 (2026-10-28)

## 15.0.0 (2026-10-28)

## 2.0.0 (2026-10-28)

The apps and web sign in and sync through jaybulb-api on Cloudflare. Along the way since 1.0.5: $1 natural voices on the web, a voice usage meter, a daily review nudge, a Watch line from your summaries, the Mac three-column layout, a mini player, Goodreads shelves that fold and sort, and a fix for listen scripts leaking between books.
