Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Bookrank is a static reading list and book-summary site. It has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no cookies, and no tracking scripts. All content is served as static files.

The app

The Bookrank iOS and macOS app reads summaries bundled with the app or synced from your own iCloud account. It sends no data to us and has no server component.

Third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard request data (IP address, user agent) as described in GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not have access to those logs.
